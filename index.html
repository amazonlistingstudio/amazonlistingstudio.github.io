Concept redesign

Same product.
Different decision.

Kitchen
Where it fell short

Low contrast buried the dishwasher-safe claim and blended the steel product into the background.

What we changed

Added a deep navy background, tripled the headline size and rebuilt the benefit block at full contrast.

Result

The benefit reads first, while the cookware remains the brightest object in the frame.

Smart home
Where it fell short

Equal bullets and a small pack shot described features, but never showed the experience.

What we changed

Replaced the feature list with a voice-command scene and enlarged the device in a real interior.

Result

Voice control is understood before the shopper reads a single bullet.

Household
Where it fell short

The slide claimed strength in text, but the loosely folded bag proved nothing.

What we changed

Added a loaded-bag demonstration, a larger claim and compact high-contrast specification chips.

Result

Load capacity is visible at a glance, without crowding the product.

Anatomy of a listing

Eight slides.
Eight reasons.

+13% Conversion on this listing
01

Win the search click

The Amazon-compliant main image shows the complete kit, cleanly scaled on white, with no graphic noise competing with the product.

02

Lead with value

Competitor research showed buyers compare versatility first. We lead with 11-in-1 and 6500 RPM so the advantage reads in seconds.

03

Show the full kit

Reviews revealed uncertainty about what arrives in the box. One organized view makes the bundle easy to understand and compare.

04

Remove the care objection

Cleaning and shower use were common purchase questions. IPX6 protection and washable heads answer both without extra explanation.

05

Make specs scannable

Battery life, charge time and size drive practical comparison. We grouped them into one fast technical check.

06

Answer the comfort fear

Review language repeatedly mentioned pulling and irritation. This frame answers the emotional objection before it becomes a lost sale.

07

Turn features into use

“11-in-1” is abstract on its own. Hair, beard, detail and body scenarios show exactly where that versatility pays off.

08

Close with ease

A clear attachment close-up proves switching functions is simple, leaving the buyer with confidence instead of another question.

Stainless steel cookware setKitchen · 5 slides
Where it fell short

A white ground, a black headline at body weight and a pale blue benefit card. The steel pot and the steel dishwasher were the same value, so the product never separated from its background.

How we got there

Cerebro first: pull every term the category actually ranks for, then check which of them a shopper can see without reading. Dishwasher safe was one of them, and the old slide only stated it, in body-weight type on white, with a steel pot sitting against a steel dishwasher. So we made it visible: ground flipped to deep navy, claim in heavy caps at roughly three times its old size in the top left, benefit block rebuilt at full contrast.

What that produced

One claim leads, three proofs stack under it, and the pot is the brightest object in the frame. The slide now survives being seen at thumbnail size.

Smart home controllerSmart home · 5 slides
Where it fell short

Three capabilities listed as equal bullets next to a small product shot. Nothing told a buyer what using the thing is actually like.

How we got there

Keyword and review work on this category keeps surfacing the same question, and it is never a spec: what is it actually like to live with. The old slide answered with three equal bullets and a thumbnail of the box. So the feature list became the interaction itself, a spoken command with the device answering, and the speaker moved into a real room at usable size.

What that produced

The slide demonstrates the product instead of describing it. A shopper understands voice control without reading a single bullet.

Heavy duty trash bagsHousehold · 5 slides
Where it fell short

A beige slide with soft yellow chips and the bag shown loosely folded. The core claim, that it holds weight, was asserted in text and shown nowhere.

How we got there

Teardown of the shelf shows which promise everyone makes and nobody proves. For bags it is strength, and the old slide asserted it in a chip while showing the product loosely folded. So we staged the proof instead: the bag hangs loaded and taut, the claim runs across the top in heavy caps, and the chips were rebuilt to survive a thumbnail.

What that produced

The strength claim is visible before it is read. Every supporting spec still fits without crowding the product.

Anatomy of a listing

Six slots.
Six jobs.

01

Main image

The only frame that runs in search results. It wins the click or nothing behind it gets read.

02

The objection

The worry that quietly stops the purchase, answered first. Here it is whether a device like this hurts.

03

The economics

Why buy this instead of the obvious alternative. One device against one visit to a clinic.

04

Proof

The claim everyone in the category makes, demonstrated on real cases instead of asserted.

05

How it works

The mechanism, for the buyer who will not spend money on something they cannot explain.

06

Who it is for

The audience beyond the obvious one. Widening who sees themselves in the product.

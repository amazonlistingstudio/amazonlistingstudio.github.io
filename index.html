Behind the design

Competitor teardown

Every listing at the top of the category: what it earns, what it charges, how many reviews it hides behind. That is where the gap shows up.

Search and keywords

What buyers actually type, which terms carry volume, and which of them have to be readable in the images themselves.

Where the gaps are

Organic against sponsored, word frequency across the category. The words everyone uses, and the ones nobody claimed yet.

Blind and A/B tests

People who have never seen the product get six seconds with the main image. If they cannot say what it does, it gets redrawn.
